POSEIDON  TEAM

Poseidon team is a research student group from the University of Western Attica that designs and builds single-seater vehicles. The team started as a simple idea in 2012 from two mechanical engineering students named Trireme, participating in competitions such as the SHELL ECO-MARATHON, while also preparing for entry into the world of the Formula Student competition.
